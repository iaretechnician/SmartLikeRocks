ONLINE SYSTEMS

A competitive management simulation of the dial-up era. Build your BBS, cultivate a community, and battle for digital supremacy in the BBS Wars.

The Engine of Growth

The core of the game is a strategic feedback loop. Every decision you make as a Sysop feeds back into the system, driving your BBS towards dominance or obscurity. Mastering this cycle of investment and return is the key to victory.

1. Invest

Allocate Capital to purchase hardware, software, and fund events.

→

2. Improve

Upgrades increase capacity, speed, and features, boosting Reputation.

→

3. Attract

A better BBS organically draws in new Users from the Area Code.

→

4. Generate

A larger user base generates more Capital, restarting the cycle.

Two Roles, One Digital Frontier

The Sysop: Community Architect

As a System Operator, your world is one of management, creation, and defense. You are the builder. Your mission is to transform a single phone line and a clunky computer into a legendary online hub, balancing technical upgrades with community hosting to attract and retain a loyal user base.

Primary Goal: Dominate the Area Code by growing your User Base and Reputation.

The Player: Digital Agent

As a Player, you are a free agent in the digital wilderness. Your world is one of exploration, investigation, and conflict. You are the catalyst. Your mission is to navigate the network, participate in communities, solve complex scavenger hunts, and engage in cyber-warfare to build your own reputation and wealth.

Primary Goal: Accumulate Capital, Skills, and Reputation to become a legendary user or launch your own BBS.

The Sysop's Playbook: A Task-Oriented Deep Dive

Daily Management Strategic Growth Community Building

Keeping the Lights On

These are the essential, hands-on tasks that consume your daily "Action Points" and keep your BBS from descending into chaos. Neglecting them will quickly erode your Reputation.

  • Validate New Users: Manually review and approve applications to filter out troublemakers and bots.
  • Moderate Message Boards: Resolve flame wars, delete spam, and enforce your board's rules to maintain a healthy community.
  • Curate File Libraries: Scan user uploads for viruses, write descriptions, and organize files into the correct directories.
  • Perform System Maintenance: Run daily backups, defragment hard drives, and monitor hardware for stress to prevent catastrophic failures.

Building the Machine

This is where you invest your hard-earned Capital to expand your BBS's capabilities, allowing it to support more users and offer better features.

Hardware Upgrades
  • Modems: Upgrade from 300 baud to 56k to slash download times.
  • Phone Lines: Add more lines to eliminate busy signals and allow more concurrent users.
  • Storage: Increase from megabytes to gigabytes to host massive file libraries.
Software & Specialization
  • BBS Software: Purchase advanced software (like WWIV or Renegade) for better features.
  • Door Games: License popular games like L.O.R.D. to attract gamers.
  • Specialization: Declare a niche (Gaming, Chat, Corporate, etc.) to gain unique bonuses.
Comparing BBS Specializations

Fostering Loyalty

A great machine is nothing without a soul. These actions are designed to build a vibrant community, generate Reputation, and make your BBS the place to be.

  • Start Discussions: Post engaging topics on message boards to stimulate conversation.
  • Host Live Events: Organize scheduled game tournaments, live chat nights, or themed parties.
  • Publish a Zine: Write and release a regular digital newsletter with BBS news and user highlights.
  • Design a Scavenger Hunt: Create a multi-stage puzzle (ARG) for your users to solve, offering a large Capital or Reputation reward for the winners.

The Player's Toolkit: Life on the Digital Edge

Standard Activities The Underground

Life on the Boards

As a user, you are free to roam the network. Your time and loyalty are valuable commodities that Sysops will compete for.

  • Community Participation: Join discussions on message boards, send private Netmail, and build your personal reputation.
  • Play Door Games: Compete in text-based games to earn Capital and bragging rights.
  • File Trading: Download files from libraries and upload your own rare finds to gain favor with the Sysop.
  • Solve Scavenger Hunts (ARGs): Collaborate with other users to solve complex, network-wide puzzles for massive rewards and prestige.

Conflict and Espionage

For those who walk a shadier path, the BBS Wars offer many opportunities. These actions are high-risk, high-reward, and form the core of player-vs-player conflict.

  • User Poaching: Act as an agent for a Sysop, spreading dissent on rival boards to lure users away.
  • Spamming Attacks: Deploy bots to flood a rival's message boards with junk, damaging their Reputation.
  • DDoS Attacks: Assemble or rent a botnet to take a competing BBS completely offline.
  • Corporate Espionage: Hack into high-security corporate BBSes to steal valuable data for profit or strategic advantage.

The BBS Wars: Offense and Defense

The central conflict of Online Systems is the constant arms race between Sysops. Every offensive action has a defensive counter, forcing players to make critical strategic decisions about how to allocate their resources. Will you be an aggressor, a fortress, or a neutral builder?

Offensive Action Effect on Target Defensive Countermeasure
Spam Attack Floods forums, causing sustained Reputation loss. Manual cleanup (costs time) or installing Anti-Spam Filter software.
User Poaching Slowly drains users by spreading dissent. Active community moderation and building high user loyalty.
DDoS Attack Takes BBS offline, causing severe Reputation and User loss. Investing in more Phone Lines or subscribing to a Traffic Scrubber service.
Corporate Data Heist Steals strategic data (e.g., user lists, source code). Installing high-end Firewalls and Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS).
